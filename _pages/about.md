## Mathematics M.S. Student & Computer Scientist

I am a first-generation college graduate with proud Mexican heritage from Albuquerque, NM. I am currently a Mathematics master's student at the University of New Mexico, and I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from UNM in 2026 and my B.S. in Computer Science from UNM in 2025.

I work as a **Computer Scientist and Researcher at Knight Scientific Systems**, where I focus on computer vision and path planning research and development.

My current mathematical interests are in **computational topology for data analysis, differential equations, and numerical analysis**. I am also an officer in the **UNM SIAM (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematicians) chapter**.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing soccer, hiking, and training for 5Ks and half marathons. I also enjoy reading dystopian fiction and writing short stories.
