I am a first-generation college graduate from Albuquerque, NM. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Mathematics at the University of New Mexico, having earned my M.S. in Computer Science in 2026 and my B.S. in Computer Science in 2025.

I work as a **Computer Scientist and Researcher at Knight Scientific Systems**, where I focus on **computer vision and path planning** research and development.

My current mathematical interests include **computational topology for data analysis, differential equations, and numerical analysis**. I am also an officer in the **UNM SIAM (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics) chapter**.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing soccer, hiking, and training for 5Ks and half marathons. I also enjoy reading dystopian fiction and writing short stories.
